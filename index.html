About

I am a researcher in wireless communications with interests in next-generation systems including 6G, terahertz communication, and index modulation. My work focuses on low-complexity and spectrally efficient communication techniques.

Research Interests

  • Index Modulation for beyond 5G/6G
  • Reconfigurable Intelligent Surfaces (RIS)
  • Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC)
  • Cell-free / Distributed MIMO
