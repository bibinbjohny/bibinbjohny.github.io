About

In research, I explore. In teaching, I connect.

I am a wireless communications researcher at Dhirubhai Ambani University, India, working on 6G systems spanning RIS, Cell-free MIMO, ISAC, and Index Modulation. A passionate educator at heart, I find as much joy in a student's curious question as in a research breakthrough — and often, one leads to the other.

Research Interests

  • Cell-free / Distributed MIMO
  • Reconfigurable Intelligent Surfaces (RIS)
  • Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC)
  • Index Modulation for beyond 5G/6G
